# How Our App Fits With Mitchell — One-Page Summary

_For the end user. Plain language, no technical background needed. Based on public information only; nothing
here has been confirmed with Mitchell yet. Last updated: 2026-11-01._

## The short version

Mitchell sells photo tools, but we are not sure the base Mitchell Connect has a photo button; it may need an
add-on. Nothing we found lets you **talk** through the damage. Our app is built around that: take a photo, tap,
say what you see, and your words appear as text.

## What Mitchell already does

- Runs in a web browser (nothing to install). It looks like it works on phones, tablets and computers.
- Offers photo tools as add-ons (such as Guided Photo Capture); whether your version includes them is a question for you.
- Can build a **first-draft estimate from photos** (an add-on called Intelligent Estimating). An estimator reviews it.
- Does the heavy lifting: parts, labor times, prices, repair procedures, scheduling.

## What our app adds

- **Scan the VIN barcode with the camera** — the app reads the barcode, fills the VIN and then looks up the year, make and model, so there's less to type. **Scan the license plate** the same way (tried on New Jersey plates, including vanity plates; always double-check it against the car). Vehicle color is a dropdown.
- **Voice dictation** — tap, talk, stop, words appear, with a note for **each photo**. Photos are numbered so a note can say "image 3."
- **GPS and time printed on every photo**, so you can show where and when it was taken.
- **The whole job in one package** (photos, notes and data in a zip) ready to email, plus a one-tap "copy all notes."
- Each damage item gets its own photos and notes. You can remove one you don't want.

## What our app does *not* do

It does not write estimates, price parts, or replace Mitchell. It is a front end for the walk-around.

## How the two could work together

| Option | What it means | Needs Mitchell's OK? |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Send a package** | The app makes a neat bundle (photos + notes + report). You email or upload it to the job in Mitchell yourself. | **No** — works now |
| **2. Our own online storage** | Photos and notes are saved on our secure server, then shared as a link or file. | No |
| **3. Direct connection** | Your inspection appears in Mitchell automatically. | **Yes** — we've asked nothing yet |

Mitchell says it has an "open platform," and the collision industry has shared rules for sending photos between
systems. Option 3 may be possible, but it needs Mitchell's agreement and likely a membership fee. **We will not try
to "get around" Mitchell's system** — only use doors they open.

## Where we are

- The app works on a computer and has been tried on an **iPhone 14**: the camera, microphone, voice-to-text and
  GPS stamp all worked. It also seemed to work on an older iPad Pro, though that was only a quick check. Since
  then the app was updated (a note for each photo, numbered photos, room for 50+ photos, and a zip package to email);
  the update is being tested before it goes to your phone. Android devices and working without an internet
  connection have not been tested yet.
- Nothing is backed up online yet. Everything stays on the one device.
- Direct connection to Mitchell is **on hold** until we know it's wanted and allowed.

## Where this could help your team

For a manager, the value is probably less about typing less and more about **proof, consistency and visibility**:

- Every photo shows **where and when** it was taken.
- Every damage item has its **photos and spoken notes together**.
- A **required-photo checklist** could stop jobs going out with missing shots.
- You could **review and approve** an inspector's work, with a map of where the photos were taken.

2. Do you take photos in Mitchell today? Where — is there a photo button in your version, or is it an add-on?

10. When the photos and notes arrive by email, what would make them easiest to use — a PDF summary with the pictures and notes next to each other?
11. Which kinds of inspection do you do? We list Initial, Supplement, Re-inspection, Final and Total loss — is that right? Anything to add or remove?
12. Does your vehicles' VIN sticker (door jamb) have a barcode? Could you send a few photos of real VIN stickers, with the barcode visible, to test the scanner with?
